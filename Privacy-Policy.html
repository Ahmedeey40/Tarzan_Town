Overview

Tarzan Town ("we", "us", "the Service") operates web and mobile services designed for fast, lightweight interactions. We collect and process personal data to operate the Service, keep it secure, and continuously improve user experience. This policy summarizes our practices and the controls available to you. For company-specific contractual terms, those agreements will take precedence where applicable.

Data We Collect

Directly provided data

  • Account information: name, email address, and hashed passwords to authenticate you securely.
  • Profile & content: files, messages, preferences, and any content you choose to upload.
  • Payment & billing: we use PCI-compliant processors; we do not store full card numbers on our servers.

Automatically collected data

  • Device and connection: IP address, device identifiers, browser type, operating system, and basic diagnostics.
  • Usage data: pages visited, feature interactions, timestamps, and crash reports to improve stability.
  • Cookies/local storage: identifiers used for session management and preferences.

Third-party sources

With your consent, we may obtain information from third-party services (e.g., social sign-in providers or analytics partners). We limit the scope of such imports and document what is shared at the time of connection.

How We Use Data

We use personal data to operate and improve Tarzan Town. Typical uses include:

  • Delivering core features, processing transactions, and communicating account notices.
  • Personalizing content and settings so the Service feels tailored to you.
  • Security and fraud prevention, including abuse detection and account protection.
  • Product improvement through aggregated analytics and controlled A/B testing.
  • Marketing communications only when you have explicitly opted-in — you may opt out anytime.
4

Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to make the Service work, measure performance, and—where permitted—deliver relevant promotions. You can manage cookie preferences through the consent banner or browser settings. Third-party analytics providers may process anonymized or pseudonymized data on our behalf.

Legal Bases (GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, we process personal data on several legal bases:

  • Contractual necessity: to provide services you requested.
  • Legitimate interests: for security, product improvement, and fraud prevention (we balance interests against your privacy).
  • Consent: for optional marketing and non-essential cookies.
  • Legal obligations: when required by law or regulatory requests.
6

Third-Party Sharing

We share data only as necessary with trusted partners:

  • Service providers: hosting, payment processors, and email providers under contract and limited access.
  • Legal and safety: to comply with lawful requests or to protect rights and safety.
  • Business transfers: in the event of a merger or sale; we will notify impacted users when appropriate.
We do not sell your personal data for marketing purposes.
7

AI & Model Use

If you enable AI-powered features, inputs you provide may be processed by secure third-party AI APIs. We strive to remove direct identifiers before external processing and will not use sensitive personal data to train public models without explicit permission.

8

Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described or to meet legal obligations. When you delete your account, we remove personal data within a defined window except where legal retention is required. Specific retention periods are available on request.

9

Security

We protect data with industry-standard measures: TLS in transit, encrypted backups, hashed passwords, and role-based access controls. Regular audits and monitoring help us detect and respond to incidents. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and authorities as required by law.

10

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise rights, contact us at the details below. We will verify requests to protect your privacy and respond within applicable timeframes.

11

Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13 (or local minimum age). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child provided personal data, contact us and we will promptly delete it.
